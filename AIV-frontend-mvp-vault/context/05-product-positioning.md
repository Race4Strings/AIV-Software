# AIV Product Positioning & Platform Requirements
## Distilled from Sales Playbook v1.0 (March 2026)
## For development reference — not the full sales doc

---

## Core Positioning

AIV is identity infrastructure for the digital twin economy. We certify, protect,
and monetize a talent's digital twin. Zero upfront cost — AIV earns a platform
commission only when licensing deals close (per-deal revenue model).

**One-liner**: "AI clones of talent are everywhere. Nobody can prove who owns them.
Nobody can license them safely. We fix all of it."

**Analogy**: "Think of it like Visa — but for AI identity."

---

## The Three Value Props (What the Platform Must Deliver)

1. **Protection** — Immutable proof-of-ownership for the digital identity.
   Traceable, enforceable. The platform must make ownership undeniable.

2. **Revenue** — Licensing deals that close in hours, not months.
   Revenue collected and distributed automatically.

3. **Control** — Talent's team sets every rule: what the twin can/can't do,
   approved brands, territories, price floors. Nothing goes live without
   explicit authorization.

---

## The Three Stages (Development Roadmap)

### Stage 1 — THE VAULT (Protect the Asset) ← CURRENT MVP
- Capture and build the digital twin (onboarding, ALCM, voice)
- Certify ownership with proof-of-creation record
- Nothing is deployed, nothing is public
- Talent reviews and approves before anything moves forward
- **Outcome**: A legally defensible, verified digital identity

### Stage 2 — THE INTAKE (Monetize the Asset) ← NEXT
- Licensing portal goes live
- Brands/buyers submit structured requests
- Manager sets rules: pricing floors, use cases, territory restrictions
- Deals auto-generate contracts, process payments, distribute revenue
- **Outcome**: Licensing deals closing in hours, not months

### Stage 3 — THE MULTIPLIER (Scale the Asset) ← FUTURE
- Twin deploys across multiple platforms simultaneously
- Marketplace opens for full buyer access
- Misuse detection goes live — unauthorized use flagged in real time
- API access for developers, games, apps, metaverse
- **Outcome**: An identity economy running on autopilot

## Market Reality (Use for Product Decisions)

- Unauthorized AI clones already circulating on social platforms, ad campaigns, apps
- No standard exists for proving who owns an AI-generated likeness
- Brands wanting to license AI likenesses have no safe, legal way to do it at scale
- NO FAKES Act, SAG-AFTRA AI provisions, EU AI Act forcing industry toward
  verifiable consent and ownership records
- Window to establish the standard: ~24 months before it gets crowded

## Key Product Implications

1. **Less AI, more control**: Don't add AI features where simple forms/workflows
   suffice. AI costs tokens; control costs nothing. Per-deal revenue model means
   minimizing operational AI costs.

2. **The Vault must feel like a vault**: Premium, secure, trustworthy. Not a
   generic SaaS dashboard. Every screen should reinforce "your identity is
   protected here."

3. **Manager experience matters**: One manager can bring 10-50 talent. The
   platform needs to support multi-talent management views eventually.

4. **Certification must feel legally meaningful**: The proof-of-creation record
   is the core product of Stage 1. It needs to look and feel like a legal
   document, not a developer hash.

5. **Training Portal = approval workflow**: Manager/talent submits ALCM changes
   → review → approve → applied. Not a chatbot conversation.

6. **Digital footprint scan**: A basic scan for unauthorized AI use of talent's
   likeness would be the most compelling demo/onboarding feature. Even a simple
   version (reverse image search, social media scan) adds massive value.

7. **No crypto/web3 language**: Avoid blockchain terminology. Use "cryptographic
   proof" and "tamper-proof record" instead. Trust > hype.
